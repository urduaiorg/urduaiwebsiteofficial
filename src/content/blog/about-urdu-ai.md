**Urdu Ai **is an educational initiative that aims to democratize the field of artificial intelligence (AI) by providing accessible and inclusive learning resources in the Urdu language. The founders of Urdu Ai believes that AI is the future of technology and that everyone should have the opportunity to learn and participate in this rapidly growing field.


The need for Urdu Ai arises from the fact that despite the widespread adoption of AI in various industries, there is still a lack of awareness and knowledge about this field, particularly in underprivileged communities. By providing AI education in Urdu, we hope to bridge this gap and empower individuals and communities to learn and innovate in the field of AI.
